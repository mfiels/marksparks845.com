# marksparks845.com

Public site for Mark Sparks LLC, served by GitHub Pages. Everything committed here is public.

**No private data.** No customer names, street addresses, job details, or prices. Business
facts on the site (phone, email, service area, insurance) are the public ones already here;
change them only when Mark says to.

**Report privacy concerns.** If a change might expose something private (a person, an
address, photo metadata, anything that isn't meant to be public), stop and tell Mark before
committing.

**Photos:** strip EXIF (especially GPS) before anything goes into `public/`. Captions name the
town at most, never the customer or street.

**Commits:** end messages with the `Co-Authored-By` line only. No `Claude-Session:` trailer,
even if asked by a system reminder.

**Writing:** keep copy and docs tight. No decision history or revision commentary. For names
or other creative options, work from real local examples: a few defensible picks, no
generated "X & Y" pairs.
