Questions

Will you fix the walls after running new wiring?

If you want me to. Wall repair is its own line on the estimate, so you can have me patch, sand, and paint to match, handle it yourself, or turn it into an accent wall while it's open.

Are you insured?

Yes, fully insured. I can send a certificate of insurance before the job starts.

Can you install a light fixture I already bought?

Yes, as long as it's UL or ETL listed. Look for the mark on the box or the label. The National Electrical Code (NEC) requires listed fixtures, and a lot of what's sold on online marketplaces isn't. If you're not sure, send me the link before you buy, or I can source one for you.

Do I need a permit?

It depends on the scope of the work. If your job needs one, I handle the application and the town inspection.

How do I get a price?

Call or text and tell me what you have in mind. A photo of the space helps. I'll follow up to talk it through or set up a time to take a look.
